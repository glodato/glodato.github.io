Research / Focus

From young planets
to massive black holes

My work connects theory, numerical simulations and observations to understand accretion disc dynamics and angular momentum transport across different scales in the Universe.

01↗

Planet-forming discs

How gas, dust and gravity shape young planetary systems — from angular-momentum transport to gravitational instability and fragmentation.

  • Protostellar discs
  • Planet formation
  • Self-gravity
02↗

Accretion dynamics

The dynamics of thin, warped and self-gravitating discs, studied through analytic models and large-scale hydrodynamical simulations.

  • Disc warps
  • Hydrodynamics
  • SPH simulations
03↗

Black holes & transients

The formation and growth of massive black holes, and the luminous transients produced when stars are tidally disrupted.

  • Tidal disruption events
  • Black holes
  • Gravitational waves
